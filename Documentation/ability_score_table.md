What a character ability is worth
==============================================================================

Every competitor except the player played every other 10 times,
with every team drawn at rating 150 so nobody's Pokemon are
better than anybody else's, and with their designed ace
thrown away as well -- so every competitor draws from an
identical distribution and the character ability is the
only thing left that differs between any two of them.

This is not who the better competitor is. It measures a
character nobody plays: the ace is half of what makes one,
and it has been deliberately discarded. --keep-aces answers
that other question.

ACE is the base-stat total of their best designed Pokemon.
It took no part in these battles and is here only to say
what each competitor gave up. Neither did RATING.

Differences under 3.5 points are variance, not ability --
measured from the competitors who share one, at the end.

A row marked (*) holds an ability this mode cannot measure:
see the note below the table.
