DOES THE SMART AI'S SWITCHING COST IT THE LOW BANDS?
==========================================================================

900 teams per band per arm, each played twice with the sides swapped.
Both sides field identical copies of one randomly drawn team, carry
the same rating, and have their character ability cleared. All three
arms fly the same teams, so the differences are paired.

Every number is the SMART side's win rate against the dumb AI.

    baseline     the smart AI as shipped
    no switch    it may never volunteer a switch (the ablation)
    dumb rule    it switches only where the dumb AI would

Generated by Test/ai_head_to_head.py in 4.8 minutes.
