What each competitor's rating is actually worth
==============================================================================

Every competitor except the player played every other competitor
5 times -- 300 matches each, 9150 battles in all -- and every result
moved both sides' ratings by the game's own formula (elo_rating() in
Scripts/Game/game_procedure.py).

Teams were rolled from each competitor's SHIPPED rating on every
match, never from the rating they were climbing to. Otherwise a
competitor who got ahead would start fielding better Pokemon and get
further ahead -- a feedback loop measuring itself. So the numbers
below are what each roster earned, at the strength it ships at.

Generated by Test/ai_rating_simulation.py in 2.2 minutes.


Read the REAL column, not the GAME column
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GAME is the engine's formula exactly, starter protection and all.
REAL is the same formula with starter protection switched off.

They differ because starter protection rounds every loss up by one,
and that has no equilibrium: a competitor winning exactly half their
matches still gains +1 per win/loss pair -- at rating 1 and at rating
750 alike. Over 300 matches that is about +150 of inflation on
everybody, which buries the signal. GAME is what a real career would
do to these numbers; REAL is what the team is worth, because without
the cushion a competitor climbs exactly until they are winning half
their matches against the field.
