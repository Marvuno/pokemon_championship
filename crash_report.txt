Background:
The most prosperous and prestigious region in the Pokemon World, known as Krusades, is organizing the World Pokemon Championship
after a resounding success last year. the winner will be renowned as the Official World Champion
With an astronomical amount of prize money (roughly 2M USD for the champion) and the fame of being the World's No.1 trainer among the world,
the Championship successfully attracted thousands of trainers around the world to challenge the title. After several stages of
Preliminaries and Wildcards chosen by the organizers, the Tournament filtered the remaining 32 elites in the Playoffs stage.
Despite the fierce competition, there are a few Tournament favorites throughout. Before the Official Pokemon Championship,
Krusades invited a few renowned trainers in an Unofficial match. Marvin is the Champion of the match and claimed himself as the World Champion,
which given his prowess is relatively uncontested and even unanimously accepted.
In the Unofficial World Pokemon Championship, he has defeated the opponent in all 5 rounds in domination almost effortlessly.
This year, he is directly seeded into the Playoffs striving to be the reigning champion. Along with World Champion Marvin,
There are 8 particularly strong participants, otherwise known as the Elite Eight. They are further divided into Lower Elite Four 四小天王 and
Upper Elite Four 四大天王, where the Upper ones each obtains an honor title. This is in accordance with the World Pokemon Rankings Table and will be
updated every few years. 

You have been renowned as one of the most talented trainers in recent decades. You are highly respected as one of the rising prodigies in the
Pokemon Competitive Environment. Most recently, you have been the youngest winner of the U15 Pokemon Amateur Tournament in the history.
Hence, the organizer generously granted you the wildcard, allowing you to directly seed into the Playoffs.
Each trainer has his/her own character ability. Your character ability is the ability to copy one Pokemon of the opponent when emerging victorious.
| 
Tutorial #1: Receiving Pokemon

If your team has less than 4 Pokemon at the start, you will receive random Pokemon from the organizer.
When you have won, you are usually allowed to take 1 Pokemon from the opponent or receive a random Pokemon instead.
However, if you have more Pokemon than needed for the next round, you will not receive any Pokemon or grant the opportunity to
take 1 Pokemon from the opponent upon victory.
If you lost the battle, unfortunately you will automatically receive a random Pokemon only.
If you already have a team of six (full team), You can swap 1 Pokemon if you won the battle, and nothing to do if you lost.
(NOTE: the Pokemon you receive initially depends on your ratings. The higher your ratings, the better Pokemon you will get!
       the Pokemon you randomly obtains when you WON the battle depends on the opponent. The stronger the opponent, the better Pokemon you will get!
       the Pokemon you randomly obtains when you LOST the battle will always be much inferior.
| 
Tutorial #2: Before Battle

The system will randomly pair 32 participants. The individual match-up will then be shown. You should see 32 of these boxes:
╔====╦======╦========╦=======╗
║ ID ║ NAME ║ POINTS ║ SCORE ║
╚====╩======╩========╩=======╝
Points: each win scores 1 point, and each defeat scores 0 point.
Score: the number of Pokemon defeated in that match. It will thus only display after the round. The purpose of it is for tiebreaks.

As the Player, you may do 4 things before entering the battle:
View My Pokemon: have a solid understanding of your team. You want to do this at least once at the start, since you will obtain new Pokemon.
Switch Pokemon Order: you may switch a Pokemon to be the starting Pokemon.
Scout Opponent: know about your next opponent. It will show his/her Character Ability and Signature Pokemon.
                If your ratings are high, you can even know about the starting Pokemon of the opponent, sometimes including its moveset!
                (TIPS: Pokemon with the ability Illuminate can increase the probability of this by 10 times, for ability Pressure 100 times.)
Check History: your record against him/her, and the scoreline of every previous meeting.
If your team has more Pokemon than the round requires, you will have to select some Pokemon that you DO NOT need for this round.
| 
Tutorial #3: In Battle

I will assume that you are a Pokemon expert when you play this game. Hence, I cannot give you much tips for the battle.
But I would like to remind you that the weather for each battle may have changed. Observe carefully.
For opponent with ratings <30, they are usually more stupid and only rely on attacking moves. Any opponent higher than that ratings can be
a prominent opponent that requires special attention.
However, if you think that your Pokemon is vastly better than your opponent, you may activate Auto Battle with '100'. Sometimes, the AI may
even perform better than you. ^_^
You may receive or swap Pokemon after the battle, depends on the situation.
| 
Tutorial #4: End Game

Very soon you will play all 5 rounds and receive the result. A large scoreboard will be displayed. However, usually only the Champion matters.
The ranking is based on 3 factors: Points, Net Kill Score and Ratings.
Points: first deciding factor. The higher the points, the higher the ranking.
Opponent Score (OS): second deciding factor. It's the accumulated points of your defeated opponents. The higher the better.Net Kill Score (NKS): third deciding factor. net number of pokemon you fainted in total.  The higher the better.
Ratings: third deciding factor. The lower the ratings, the higher the ranking.
You will also see how many ratings you gain in this Tournament.
(NOTE: Initially, you may find it difficult to gain ratings. No worries. After some time, you will grasp some win by having a mediocre team.
       When you slowly build up your ratings, it will trigger a snowball effect and you will start having better performance.)

That's all for the tutorial! Please enjoy the game for now!
